What affects the price within each range

The final quote depends on your situation. Typical factors include:

  • The size and complexity of your website
  • How competitive your market is
  • The number of locations or services
  • The current condition of your site and profile
  • How much work is needed each month
  • Your goals and timeline

After a short conversation about your business, I will give you a clear quote. A good first step is the free 15-minute review.

Pricing questions

Which plan should I start with?

If you are not sure what is holding your website back, start with the Growth Audit and Roadmap. It gives you a clear picture and a plan, and you can then decide whether to continue with monthly support.

Why are the prices shown as ranges?

Every website and market is different. A range lets you plan, and I confirm the exact price after understanding your business and goals.

Do you guarantee rankings or leads?

No. SEO is a long-term activity, and results are affected by competition, search-engine changes, website quality and market conditions. My focus is a structured, data-informed strategy and honest reporting of progress.

Can I change plans later?

Yes. Many clients begin with an audit and move to a monthly plan once they have seen the roadmap.
